Account deletion

Atrium Games SL  ·  Last updated: July 2026

How to delete your account

You can delete your Atrium account at any time from Profile → Account settings → Delete account. This starts the deletion process immediately.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your login and profile are permanently removed, and you won't be able to sign back in.
  • Any room you're hosting stays open for the other players in it — we remove you as host rather than closing the room out from under them.
  • Records of games you've played are kept so that other players' game history stays intact, but they no longer identify you — your account isn't linked to them once deletion completes.
  • Requests you've made (like asking us to add a game) are removed.

Some information may take a short time to fully clear from our systems as part of normal processing, but your account and personal data are removed as described above once deletion completes.

If you can't access the app

Email support@playatrium.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm once it's done.
