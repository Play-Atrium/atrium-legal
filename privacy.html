Privacy policy

Atrium Games SL  ·  Last updated: May 2026

We built Atrium to bring people together around card games. This policy explains what data we collect when you use Atrium, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. We keep this as plain as we can — you deserve to understand it.

Who we are

Data controller: Atrium Games SL

Contact: hello@playatrium.com

Registered address: Calle Oropéndola 8, Las Rozas de Madrid, Madrid, 28232

What data we collect

If you use the app without an account (unregistered user)

  • An anonymous session identifier, created automatically by our authentication system
  • A temporary display name, generated automatically
  • Game session history for the duration of your session
  • Age confirmation (whether you have confirmed you are 13 or older)

If you create an account (registered user)

  • Your email address (if you sign up via email magic link)
  • Your name as provided by Apple or Google (if you sign in via Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In)
  • Your chosen display name
  • Game session history
  • Accessibility preferences you set in the app
  • Subscription and trial status

Automatically collected data

  • Anonymised usage events (which screens you visit, which games you browse) via PostHog, our analytics provider. PostHog is configured to route data through EU servers only.
  • Device identifiers for ad personalisation, if you have not purchased an Atrium Pass (see Advertising below)
  • IP address (used to route your connection; not stored persistently by us)

During gameplay

  • Keystrokes and game inputs are processed in real time to run the game. They are not stored after your session ends.

Why we collect it and our legal basis

  • To provide the service — running game sessions, managing rooms, saving your history. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To improve Atrium — anonymised analytics help us understand which games people enjoy and where the experience breaks down. Legal basis: legitimate interests.
  • To serve ads — if you are on the free tier, we show ads between games using Google AdMob. Legal basis: legitimate interests, subject to your consent preferences as presented at first launch.
  • To comply with the law — we retain certain records as required by applicable law. Legal basis: legal obligation.

Advertising

Atrium is free to use. If you are on the free tier, we show ads between games — never during play. We use Google AdMob as our ad provider. AdMob may use device identifiers to show you relevant ads.

If you purchase an Atrium Pass, all ads are removed. Atrium Pass holders, trial users, and promotional accounts are never shown ads.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers. We do not share your name, email, or game history with any ad network.

Age requirements

Atrium is rated 13 and older. You must confirm you are at least 13 to use the app. The After Dark section of the catalogue — which contains games involving drinking forfeits or mature themes — is restricted to users who confirm they are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at hello@playatrium.com and we will delete it promptly.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers necessary to run Atrium:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Data is stored in the EU.
  • PostHog — anonymised analytics, EU Cloud.
  • Google AdMob — ad serving for free-tier users.
  • ChottuLink — deep link generation for session invitations.
  • Apple / Google — if you use Sign-In with Apple or Google Sign-In, those providers process your authentication.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How long we keep your data

  • Anonymous session data is deleted when you clear the app or your session expires.
  • Account data is kept for as long as your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, all data associated with it is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.
  • Anonymised, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked to you.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Access — you can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen in the app. We will action this immediately.
  • Portability — you can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — you can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@playatrium.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

Cookies and tracking

Atrium is a mobile app and does not use browser cookies. On the website (playatrium.com), we may use essential cookies to operate the site. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising on the website.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (TLS) and access controls on our database. No system is perfectly secure — if you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at hello@playatrium.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last updated.
